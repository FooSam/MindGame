## 繁體中文版 (Traditional Chinese)

### 1. 簡介與前言
感謝您使用「左右腦鍛鍊」（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）。我們極度重視您的隱私權與個人資料保護。本隱私權政策旨在說明本應用程式在您使用過程中的資料處理方式。

### 2. 個人資料收集與使用 (Zero Data Collection)
- **零個人資料收集**：本應用程式為**純單機離線益智遊戲**，我們**不會收集、儲存、傳輸、追蹤或共享**任何使用者的個人身分識別資訊 (PII)，包含但不限於您的姓名、電子郵件、電話號碼、精確或概略地理位置、裝置識別碼 (IMEI / Android ID)、通訊錄、相簿或檔案。
- **無帳號系統**：本應用程式無需註冊或登入帳號，所有功能均可直接暢玩。

### 3. 本地資料儲存 (On-Device Storage Only)
- 本應用程式中產生的所有遊戲成績、排行榜紀錄、自訂玩家暱稱、風格主題偏好與音效設定，**全數僅儲存在您手機的本地資料庫 (SQLite / Room) 中**。
- 這些數據絕不會上傳至任何雲端伺服器或第三方遠端伺服器。若您解除安裝本應用程式或清除應用程式資料，本地儲存的成績紀錄將會被重置。

### 4. 權限說明 (App Permissions)
本應用程式僅使用運行所必需的最低系統權限：
- **震動權限 (VIBRATE)**：僅用於在遊戲點擊錯誤時提供觸覺震動反饋，提升遊戲體驗。
- 本應用程式**不要求**相機、麥克風、位置、聯絡人或外部儲存空間等任何敏感權限。

### 5. 兒童隱私保護 (Children's Privacy)
本應用程式為全齡適宜之益智腦力訓練軟體，不含任何不當內容、暴力情節或廣告追蹤，完全符合《兒童線上隱私保護法》(COPPA) 及 Google Play 家庭政策規範。

## English Version

### 1. Introduction
Thank you for using **MindGame (Brain Training)** (referred to as "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our App handles information when you use our services.

### 2. Zero Data Collection Policy
- **No Personal Information Collected**: The App is a **standalone offline puzzle game**. We **do not collect, store, transmit, track, or share** any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, device identifiers, contacts, photos, or files.
- **No Account Required**: The App does not require account creation or login to access any features.

### 3. On-Device Local Data Storage
- All game progress, best scores, leaderboard records, player nicknames, sound preferences, and visual theme settings are **stored strictly on your local device (SQLite / Room Database)**.
- None of your data is ever transmitted to external servers or third-party cloud platforms. Uninstalling the App or clearing its app data will reset all locally stored scores.

### 4. App Permissions
The App requests only minimal permissions strictly necessary for its features:
- **Vibrate (`android.permission.VIBRATE`)**: Used solely for haptic feedback upon incorrect tap interactions.
- The App **does not require** access to camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage.

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Our App contains no inappropriate content, violence, or ad-tracking scripts, making it suitable for all age groups in full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

### 6. Policy Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised effective date.
